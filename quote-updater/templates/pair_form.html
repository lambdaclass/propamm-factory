pricing
{% let field = delta %}{% include "field.html" %}

This is the whole thing. Every block we compute two numbers and send them to the chain: the spread, and the mid. The pool then sells at mid + spread/2 and buys at mid − spread/2.

spread = γ σ² τterm 1 + (2/γ) ln(1 + γ/k)term 2 + κ σ √Δterm 3 + λ σ √τ · q_outcharge, one side
mid = mid · (1 − skew) , skew = q γ σ √τskew

q_out is how far the vault's WETH share is outside inventory_band_lower to inventory_band_upper, divided by target_share. It is 0 inside that range. The charge only goes on the trade that pushes us further off target; the other price stays where it would be without it. There are two nested bands: past the inner one the charge grows at λ_soft (inventory_aversion); past the wider hard band [inventory_band_hard_lower, inventory_band_hard_upper] it grows at λ_hard (inventory_aversion_hard), so it bites much harder deep in the tail. A blank hard band edge turns that side's hard tier off; leave the hard fields blank to keep the old single-band behaviour.

σ is how much the price has been moving: take the last 600 seconds of our mid, compute each second's percentage change, take the standard deviation. For USDC/USDT it is basically 0, so term 1, term 3 and the skew are 0 and the spread is term 2, a constant. That is a fixed spread.

{% let field = volatility_window_secs %}{% include "field.html" %}
γ σ² τ term 1: we hold WETH for 12 seconds and it can drop

Someone sells us WETH at 2999. We cannot sell it until the next block, 12 seconds later, and by then the price might be 2980. This term charges extra to cover that. σ²τ is how much the price typically moves in 12 seconds. γ is how much of that to charge for; we pick it.

{% let field = gamma %}{% include "field.html" %} {% let field = hold_secs %}{% include "field.html" %}
(2/γ) ln(1 + γ/k) term 2: our profit

Terms 1 and 3 cover what we expect to lose. This one is what we earn. Charge more and fewer people trade with us; charge less and more do. k is a guess for how fast people leave when we charge more. Pick it from the spread you want: with gamma 0.1, k = 2000 gives 0.05% per side, k = 700 gives 0.14%.

{% let field = k %}{% include "field.html" %}
κ σ √Δ term 3: the price already moved and a bot trades on our old price

We post "buy at 2999". Four seconds later the market drops to 2990. A bot sells us WETH at 2999 that is worth 2990; we lost 9. σ√Δ is how much the price typically moves in the Δ seconds between us posting and someone trading. κ is how much of that to charge for; check it against how much the price actually moved after our fills.

{% let field = kappa %}{% include "field.html" %} {% let field = fill_delay_secs %}{% include "field.html" %}
skew = q γ σ √τ skew: do not sit on a big pile of WETH while it is jumpy

Say the market is at 3000 and we sell WETH at 3003 and buy it at 2997. If we are holding too much WETH and WETH is moving a lot, we lower both: sell at 3002, buy at 2996. People buy the cheap WETH from us and stop selling us more, so we end up holding less of it. If we are holding too little, we raise both. "Too much" means more than target_share of the vault's value is in WETH. If WETH is not moving, we do not do this, even if the vault is lopsided.
